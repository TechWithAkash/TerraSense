# India-specific field evidence

Source excerpts: ICAR-CRIDA District Contingency Plans for Rainfed Agriculture, 2021; ICAR NICRA
Technology Demonstration Reports, 2020; National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture Operational
Guidelines, Ministry of Agriculture, 2020; India-WRIS Groundwater Year Book, Central Ground Water Board,
2022.

## Water harvesting structures

ICAR-CRIDA's district contingency plans for rainfed and semi-arid districts document that contour
bunding and staggered trenching on sloped plots measurably raise in-season soil moisture retention and
reduce runoff-driven water table decline, typically visible within the first monsoon season after
construction.

## Irrigation efficiency

NICRA technology demonstrations comparing drip irrigation retrofits against flood irrigation on
comparable rainfed-transitioning plots found higher effective root-zone soil moisture and measurably
slower groundwater draw-down under drip systems, since less water is lost to evaporation and deep
percolation beyond the root zone.

## Diversification and soil health

The National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture's operational guidelines report that diversifying away
from continuous single-crop rotations improves soil microbial diversity and reduces cumulative nutrient
runoff relative to monoculture systems, consistent with the broader soil biota literature.

## Groundwater context

The Central Ground Water Board's groundwater year book tracks district-level water table trends across
India. In low-rainfall districts where irrigation depends heavily on borewells, a declining water table
is associated with reduced effective soil moisture during dry months, since farmers increasingly ration
or reduce irrigation as extraction becomes costlier or wells run dry.
